🎀 Quick Party Planning Tips

  • Order tableware at least a week early — Hello Kitty sets sell out fast near peak party season.
  • Mix large and small balloons at different heights for a fuller, more festive look.
  • Use the pink tablecloth as your anchor and build your color palette — pink, white, and red — around it.
  • Don't forget a "Birthday Girl" sash or crown to match the badge for extra cute photo ops.
  • Grab a few extra napkins — they double as backdrop decoration pinned to a foam board.
